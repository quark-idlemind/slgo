profile prints what somebody's profile says about them: when the
account was made, what it says about payment, who its partner is, the
web page and the text they wrote, what they say they are looking for,
can do and speak, and the groups they chose to show.  It is a public
question, answered by the grid to anybody who asks, and nothing about
it reaches the person asked about.

Who is named by a name, by a key, or by the number beside a name in the
last listing.  The fields that are always there say what they do not
know rather than printing an empty column, because a blank beside
"born" reads as an answer that went missing on the way where "not said"
reads as a profile that has none.  Four are printed only where there is
something in them -- the web page, and the "wants", "skills" and
"speaks" that viewers stopped showing years ago -- since a line of "not
said" for each of those on every profile would say nothing to anybody.

The keys stay in the lines.  A key is what everything else here takes,
including this command's own argument, so a partner or a group worth
reading about is one more line to type rather than a search.

## This is the one command that searches the grid for a name

Everywhere else, a name is matched against what the session has heard
and then against whoever is standing in the region, and no further.
This one goes on to the grid's own search, because a profile is exactly
the question one asks about somebody who is not here -- and because
guessing wrong costs nothing but a wasted listing, where guessing wrong
for im, offer or give delivers something to a stranger.  Several people
answering to the name are listed, numbered, and a number picks one.

## Two fields that are easy to misread

"none on file" and "not revealed" are different facts about payment: an
account carrying a caption -- a Linden, or one of the special accounts
-- has its payment information withheld rather than absent, and the
flags for it say nothing at all rather than no.  Where there is caption
text, it is printed under the payment line as a field of its own called
"account", so that it does not read as a title somebody chose for
themselves.

Where there is not, the payment line stands alone.  Governor Linden's
profile reads "not revealed" with no account line under it, because a
Linden is known by the number the caption comes with and the text
beside that number is empty.  So a "not revealed" with nothing
explaining it is the answer rather than a field that went missing on
the way.

The groups are only the ones their owner published, which is why the
count says so: somebody may be in a dozen and show none.

## A key nobody knows is a sentence, not a failure

The grid has no way of saying it has never heard of somebody: it
answers with an empty group list and simply never sends the profile.
So the answer for a made-up key is the wait running out, printed as the
plain sentence it is -- nothing went wrong, and the question was
answered.  It is also the only case where the wait is spent, since a
profile that exists arrives in milliseconds.

## Options

--wait, or -w, is how many seconds to give the grid, fifteen when it is
not said.  It matters only for the case above, where the whole of it is
spent before there is anything to say.

## Examples

    profile Quark Idlemind
    lookup another
    profile 2

See also: lookup for the search this makes on its own, who for the
people in the region, and group for the groups this avatar is in, which
is a different question from the ones a profile lists.
