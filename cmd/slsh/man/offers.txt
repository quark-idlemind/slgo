offers lists the friendship and inventory offers waiting for an answer.
"accept" and "decline" are what answer them, and "waiting" is the newer
listing that has these in it along with everything else somebody can be
asked.

It takes nothing.  Both kinds are in one list because from where a
person sits they are the same thing -- somebody offered something and
it has not been answered.  Each line begins with what it is, and the
two kinds fill the rest of the line differently, because they have
different things worth saying:

	friendship  Example Resident             12s ago
	object      a lantern                    4s ago   from Another Resident

A friendship offer has no name of its own, so the second column is
whoever made it; an item has one, so the second column is what it was
called and the sender comes at the end.  The names above are invented,
as everywhere in these pages.

Nothing is answered by being looked at.  A viewer draws an offer on the
screen and leaves it there; a shell has none, so an offer nobody
answers simply stays pending, and the other side is told nothing either
way.

# It can only list what arrived while this shell was here

Offers reach the client that is attached at the moment they arrive.
This shell subscribes when it starts, so anything offered before that
is not in this list -- and it is still open on the other side, which is
the awkward part: they are waiting, and nothing here shows it.

The same follows for two shells on one avatar.  The daemon hands the
offer to every client that asked for instant messages, so both hold it,
and each holds its own copy: answering in one takes it out of that
one's list and leaves the other's alone for as long as that shell
lives.  Looking again does not help, because there is nothing to look
at -- the list is what this process caught, not something the grid can
be asked for a second time.

Answering through "waiting" has the same hole nearer home.  "answer N"
and "no N" on an inventory offer send the answer and leave the offer in
place, so it is still in this listing afterwards and "accept" will
happily send a second answer to an offer already taken up; measured,
"answer 1" on a lone item printed that it was taken and "offers"
printed the item again immediately after.  A friendship offer answered
the same way does leave both listings.  Until that is fixed, answering
a thing twice is the mistake to watch for, and it is the listing that
is wrong rather than the answer.

A friendship offer can only ever be answered by the client that
received it, since the answer has to quote the identifier the offer
arrived with and there is no way to recover it afterwards.  That is why
these are kept at all rather than printed once and forgotten.

# Examples

	offers

Answer one of them by name:

	offers
	accept lantern

See also: accept and decline for answering, waiting for the same offers
alongside teleports, dialogs, permissions and group invitations, and
offer or give for the ones going the other way.
