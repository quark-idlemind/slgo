offers lists the friendship and inventory offers waiting for an answer.
"accept" and "decline" are what answer them, and "waiting" is the newer
listing that has these in it along with everything else somebody can be
asked.

It takes nothing.  Both kinds are in one list because from where a
person sits they are the same thing -- somebody offered something and
it has not been answered -- and each line says what it is, what it is
called, how long it has been waiting, and for an item who sent it.

Nothing is answered by being looked at.  A viewer draws an offer on the
screen and leaves it there; a shell has none, so an offer nobody
answers simply stays pending, and the other side is told nothing either
way.

# It can only list what arrived while this shell was here

Offers reach the client that is attached at the moment they arrive.
This shell subscribes when it starts, so anything offered before that
is not in this list -- and it is still open on the other side, which is
the awkward part: they are waiting, and nothing here shows it.

The same follows for two shells on one avatar.  Each has its own idea
of what is waiting, and answering in one does not update the listing in
the other until it looks again.

A friendship offer can only ever be answered by the client that
received it, since the answer has to quote the identifier the offer
arrived with and there is no way to recover it afterwards.  That is why
these are kept at all rather than printed once and forgotten.

# Examples

	offers

Answer one of them by name:

	offers
	accept lantern

See also: accept and decline for answering, waiting for the same offers
alongside teleports, dialogs, permissions and group invitations, and
offer or give for the ones going the other way.
